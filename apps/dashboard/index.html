How your garden grows

Each invited learner has their own patch. Complete your daily word goal to add one growth step. Plants grow from seeds to sprouts, leaves, and finally flowers or a harvest.

Every seven days in your longest streak unlocks another box. Miss a day? Your plants stay. Come back whenever you’re ready.

A little breathing room: after a completed day, one word can protect your existing streak for one day. It doesn’t add a streak day or grow a plant. Finish your goal the next day to keep going.

A shared space
